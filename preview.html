Community Policy

UnibenPlug is for students to buy, sell and get help near campus. By posting you agree to these rules.

No fraud, no illegal items, no adult content, no spam. All listings must have real photos and real prices. Violators will be removed without refund. Every post needs a real price and the right category.

Posts that break these rules are removed automatically. Three removed posts ban your account. Posts reported three times are hidden until an admin reviews them. New sellers have their first two posts reviewed by an admin.

Prototype: posts live in this page only. The live app connects to Supabase.
